# Code of conduct

WPrism welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of WordPress,
PHP, Git, and open-source experience. These expectations apply to repository
discussions, issues, reviews, and other spaces maintained for the project.

## Working together

- Discuss the change and its evidence. Explain disagreement without attacking
  the person proposing it.
- Give newcomers enough context to act on feedback. A small reproduction or
  documentation correction is a useful contribution.
- Respect people's names, identities, privacy, and time.
- Credit others' work and follow the project's contribution and licensing rules.
- Accept a maintainer's request to pause a heated exchange.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, impersonation,
publication of private information, and repeated unwanted contact are not
acceptable. Neither are deliberately disruptive comments or retaliation
against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting and moderation

For private reports of abusive content or accounts, use GitHub's
[Report abuse process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines#reporting-an-issue).
Do not repeat private information or threats in a public issue. Security
vulnerabilities have their own private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, lock a discussion, restrict
participation, or remove project access. They should explain the relevant rule
and the action to the affected person when doing so does not disclose someone
else's private information or increase the risk of harm. Serious threats or
repeated harassment can justify immediate removal.

Moderation should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on restoring a
workable community. A maintainer personally involved in a dispute should seek
another administrator's review when one is available. Reports concerning
maintainers can also be made directly to GitHub through its private reporting
process.

This policy does not require a contributor to disclose personal circumstances,
accept unwanted contact, or resolve harassment in public.
