# Community roadmap

WPrism's first public milestone is a useful alpha for WordPress agencies and
developers. The initial workflow is a non-destructive change to supported
authored content or configuration, reviewed through Git, with protected live
data preserved. The exact supported scope continues to come from the
[capability sources](capabilities.md), not this roadmap.

## Current priorities

1. **A first successful evaluation.** Short setup instructions, a disposable
   page-change demo, readable help, and clear hosting prerequisites.
2. **Agency calibration.** At least three sites across two agencies, including
   transactionally active single-site WooCommerce installations. Observe
   refusals and initially unsupported requested surfaces as well as successes.
3. **Repeatable operation.** Reduce the operator assistance needed for a second
   change, upgrade, and recovery rehearsal. Document the remaining work.
4. **Independent validation.** At least six sites across three additional
   agencies, using thresholds frozen after calibration and before validation.
5. **Contributor participation.** Reproducible bugs, useful documentation,
   representative fixtures, and bounded adapter contributions.

These are milestones, not claims of completed field evidence or promised
release dates. The [product specification](product-spec.md#phase-a--field-grounding-and-adoptability)
defines the calibration and validation cohorts. Public source availability can
precede production-readiness claims; those claims still require its launch gate.

## What we measure

- Time and assistance needed to complete the disposable demo.
- Host eligibility and the reason an intended operation is refused.
- Time to assess a site and produce a usable rehearsal environment.
- Operator decisions, qualification effort, and repeat-use success.
- Preserved runtime state, truthful refusal, and exercised recovery boundaries.
- Upgrade and requalification effort on real plugin stacks.
- Whether agencies find the outcome valuable enough to use again.

Observations are voluntary. Do not submit customer database dumps, credentials,
or identifying business data. Share minimal synthetic reproductions and
sanitized summaries; an agency's site or case study is published only with its
permission.

## Help shape the work

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/duotronic-ai/wprism/discussions) to describe
a workflow or offer a pilot. Use [Issues](https://github.com/duotronic-ai/wprism/issues)
for a reproducible defect or a specific proposed improvement.

Adapter demand is prioritized by observed user workflows and maintainable
evidence. New plugin counts alone are not an adoption result. Broader hosting,
a public adapter registry, and managed services depend on that field evidence.

[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) is the contributor starting point.
The [owner roadmap](roadmap.md) retains the detailed engineering history and
standing architectural decisions.
